Cold showers, water dripping from the ceiling or a geyser that keeps tripping the power are all signs your geyser needs attention. Left alone, a small fault can quickly become a burst geyser, damaged ceilings and a big insurance claim. Our local plumbers repair geysers across Roodepoort and the West Rand for homes, landlords and businesses.

Common geyser problems we fix:
- No hot water or lukewarm water
- Leaking or burst geysers
- Faulty elements and thermostats
- Geyser tripping the DB board
- Dripping overflow or pressure valves
- Damaged drip trays and vacuum breakers
- Noisy, banging or rumbling geysers

Warning signs to watch for:
1. Water stains or bulging on the ceiling below the geyser.
2. Water constantly dripping from the overflow pipe outside.
3. Rusty or discoloured hot water.
4. Hot water running out much faster than before.

If your geyser bursts, switch it off at the DB board, close the cold-water supply to the geyser and call us right away.
