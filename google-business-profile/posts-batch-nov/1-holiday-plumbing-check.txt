Going away for the December holidays? Give your plumbing a quick check first.

Nobody wants to come home from the coast to a burst geyser or a flooded kitchen. A few minutes before you leave can save you a lot of stress:

- Switch off your geyser at the DB board if the house will be empty.
- Close the main water valve, or at least the valves under sinks and toilets.
- Look for drips under basins and around the geyser drip tray.
- Clear leaves out of outside drains and gutters, because summer storms hit hard here.

If you spot a slow leak, a stiff stopcock or a geyser that's been dripping from the overflow pipe, now is the time to sort it out, not in the middle of your holiday.
