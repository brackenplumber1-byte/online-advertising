Drip. Drip. Drip.

A dripping tap is easy to ignore until you work out how much water it wastes. Even a slow drip can add up to hundreds of litres a month, and in a dry season that's water none of us can spare.

Most dripping taps just need a new washer, cartridge or O-ring. Sometimes the tap is simply worn out and it makes more sense to replace it, and we'll tell you honestly which one it is.

Tysons Plumbers fixes and replaces kitchen, bathroom and garden taps across Roodepoort and the West Rand. We can also fit a new mixer if you're ready for an upgrade.
