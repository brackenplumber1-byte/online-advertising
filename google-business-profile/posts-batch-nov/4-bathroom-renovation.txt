Planning a bathroom makeover in Roodepoort? Get the plumbing right first.

New tiles and a beautiful vanity look great, but it's what's behind the walls that decides whether your bathroom lasts. Shower waterproofing, the right falls to the drain, solid connections and good water pressure all matter long after the builders leave.

Tysons Plumbers works with homeowners and builders on bathroom renovations across the West Rand. We can:
- Move or install new shower, bath and basin points
- Fit mixers, rain showers and new taps
- Install close-coupled or wall-hung toilets
- Replace old galvanised pipes while the walls are open

We'll walk through your plans with you, point out anything that could cause trouble later and give you a clear quote before work starts.
