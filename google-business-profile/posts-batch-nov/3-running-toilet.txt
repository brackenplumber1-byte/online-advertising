That toilet that keeps running? It's costing you more than you think.

A cistern that never quite stops filling can waste thousands of litres a month, and you'll only notice when the water bill arrives. The good news is that it's usually a quick fix: a worn flush valve, a tired inlet valve or a seal that has had enough.

Here's a simple test. Put a few drops of food colouring in the cistern and wait 15 minutes without flushing. If the colour shows up in the bowl, water is leaking through.
