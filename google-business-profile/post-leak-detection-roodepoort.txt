Water bill suddenly higher in Roodepoort? You could have a hidden leak.

Many leaks never show on the surface. Water can escape from pipes under the slab, inside walls or underground in the garden for months before you see any sign, and all that time you are paying for it. Tysons Plumbers finds and repairs hidden leaks for homes, complexes and businesses across Roodepoort and the West Rand.

Signs you may have a hidden leak:
- Your water bill is higher than usual
- The water meter keeps turning when every tap is closed
- Damp patches, bubbling paint or mould on walls
- Warm spots on tiled floors
- Wet or extra-green patches in the garden
- Low water pressure

Quick check you can do yourself:
1. Close every tap and make sure no toilet is filling.
2. Take a photo of your water meter reading.
3. Wait 30 minutes without using any water.
4. Check the meter again. If the reading has changed, you probably have a leak.
