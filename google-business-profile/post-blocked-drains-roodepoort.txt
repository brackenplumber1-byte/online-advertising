Blocked drain in Roodepoort? Tysons Plumbers clears it fast.

A slow sink, a gurgling toilet or water pooling around an outside drain are early warning signs. Ignore them and a small blockage can become an overflowing drain, a bad smell through the house or even sewage backing up into your home. Tysons Plumbers unblocks drains across Roodepoort and the West Rand for homes, complexes and businesses.

Drains we unblock:
- Kitchen sinks and basins
- Showers and baths
- Toilets
- Outside gullies and yard drains
- Main sewer lines
- Stormwater drains

Common causes of blocked drains:
1. Fat, oil and grease poured down the kitchen sink.
2. Hair and soap build-up in showers and baths.
3. Wet wipes, sanitary products and paper towels flushed down the toilet.
4. Tree roots growing into underground pipes.
5. Leaves, sand and debris washed into outside drains.

We use professional drain machines and high-pressure jetting to clear the blockage properly, not just push it further down the pipe. If a drain keeps blocking, we find out why and recommend a lasting fix. You get a clear quote before we start.

Tip: avoid harsh chemical drain cleaners. They seldom clear a serious blockage and can damage older pipes.
